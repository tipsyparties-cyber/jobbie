grows with you

From doing it all yourself to a business that runs itself.

Doing it all yourself

One person selling and delivering. Instant quotes, online booking, one inbox and follow-ups stop the admin eating your evenings.

Owner-operator

You still deliver, with a few helpers. Shift offers, your team’s pay and tips, checklists and a client portal.

A team

You run the business and a team delivers. Hiring and onboarding, the Command Centre, reports and AI replies.

It runs itself

You handle the exceptions. Automations you set, AI that drafts and asks, and alerts when something needs a person.

Every business on Heyday can grow this way, from one person doing every job to a team run from one system.
