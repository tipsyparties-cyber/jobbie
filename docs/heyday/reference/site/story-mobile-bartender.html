what hurt most

Every booking meant dozens of steps: the quote, the chasing, the staff, the kit, the pay. Most of them were done by hand.

growth stages

Four stages, one system.

doing it all yourself

One person, every job.

It started with one person delivering the cocktail class. Instant quotes and online booking meant customers could price and book a party without a phone call.

Instant quotesOnline booking and instant bookPayments and deposits

owner-operator

Help, without a group chat.

As bookings grew, shifts went out as offers that freelance bartenders accept or decline, with tips shared through a QR tip jar.

Shift offers and staffingTips by QR and tapChecklists, shopping and kit lists

a team

A team of bartenders.

Hiring, checks and onboarding run in the same system, and one inbox holds every email, WhatsApp, text and call, with AI drafting the replies.
