what it’s costing you

What slow quotes cost you.

Example numbers

Example numbers: a business sends 40 quotes a month and usually replies the next day; an average booking is $1,200. Booking rates from Flashquotes’ data: 22% for quotes sent within 4 hours, 18% later the same day, 16% the next day or later.

Next day: 40 × 16% = 6.4 bookings. Within 4 hours: 40 × 22% = 8.8 bookings. 8.8 − 6.4 = 2.4 extra bookings a month. 2.4 × $1,200 = $2,880.

$2,880 a month, or $34,560 a year, lost to slow quotes.
