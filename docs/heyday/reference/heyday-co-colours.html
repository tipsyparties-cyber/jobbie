heyday.co colour combos

Your board, turned into eleven combinations. Each block on heyday.co gets one: a ground, a bright partner for the big type, the shapes and the wordmark, and sometimes a third colour for buttons. It's the deep, saturated cousin of jobbie's pastels, so the two sites feel related without looking the same.

Hex values picked by eye from your board. If you have the exact codes from the pins, send them and I'll swap them in.

{ how it ties to jobbie }

Same colour families, turned up

Most of heyday.co's colours are a deeper or brighter version of one of jobbie's. Pink, olive, burgundy and cherry are heyday.co's own. jobbie's colours stay exactly as they are; this is only to show how the two sites relate.

Sage→Mint and forest
Lavender→Lilac and purple
Yellow→Butter and lemon
Blue and sky→Cobalt and navy
Orange→Coral

{ the rules }

Keeping it bold, not busy

  • One combination per block, and never the same one twice in a row.
  • Three colours at most in a block, plus ink.
  • Photos sit on the light blocks (stone, mint, butter, lilac, lemon). The dark blocks (forest, burgundy, olive, navy, cherry) carry big type and shapes.
  • Big type, shapes and the wordmark use the partner colour. Body text uses whatever passes on that ground (the chip says which).
  • Buttons: "Find experiences", "Book" and "Check dates" are Heyday orange with ink text. Where orange would clash (cherry, and the blocks whose partner is coral), they're ink with cream text. Every other button takes the block's third colour, or its partner. Same size, outline and rounding hover as jobbie.
  • More info: on heyday.co the shape grows in its partner colour, and the text on it switches to whichever of ink or cream reads.
