# HoneyBook inventory (competitor comparison)

Researched 13 Sep 2026 from honeybook.com (home and nav, every /product page, the pricing page and its plan-by-plan comparison table, every integration page in the sitemap, industry pages, ai-information), help.honeybook.com (all 14 collections and their article lists, plus key articles) and the Apple App Store listing. WebSearch was out of budget; everything came from the pages directly. The pricing comparison table's ticks were parsed from the page HTML, column by column.

**Plan key:** All = Starter, Essentials and Premium. E+ = Essentials and Premium. P = Premium only. "Not stated" = no plan given anywhere I found.
**Plan prices (US, billed yearly):** Starter $29/mo, Essentials $49/mo ("Most Popular"), Premium $109/mo. Billed monthly: Starter $36, Essentials $59, Premium $129. These come from the homepage's monthly toggle, and the App Store in-app purchases agree ($35.99 / $58.99 / $128.99). 30-day free trial with no card; 60-day money-back guarantee. Sold in the US, Canada, UK and Australia, with prices in USD, CAD, GBP and AUD.
**Confirmed?** "Yes" = seen on a HoneyBook page or help article. "UNCONFIRMED" = mentioned but not backed up, or contradicted elsewhere; the reason is given.

Common URLs: PRICING = https://www.honeybook.com/pricing, HC = https://help.honeybook.com/en/ (help-centre article titles quoted).

## Contradictions found on HoneyBook's own pages
- **Card fee:** the comparison table, payments page and teams page say "2.9% + 25¢". The pricing page's fee section and FAQ say "Card fees start at 2.7% + 10¢", which is the Tap to Pay in-person rate on the payments page and in the help centre. Amex, Discover and cards on file are 3.4% + 9¢ (ai-information).
- **Free trial:** 30 days (pricing, ai-information) vs "free for 7 days" (for-teams FAQ).
- **Contracts signed:** 10M+ vs 28M+. **$23B+** is described as "projects booked" on most pages but "payments processed" on the teams page; the homepage's payments figure is $12B+.
- **Expense tracking:** the comparison table shows it on Essentials and Premium only; the help article says all plans get the Expenses page (bank import is Essentials and Premium only).
- **Passing fees to clients:** the payments FAQ says there is no built-in way; ai-information says venues can pass them on where permitted.
- **Stripe and Square** are listed as integrations, but the payments FAQ says HoneyBook processes all card and ACH payments itself.
- **Time tracking** is still advertised on the teams page but is being retired according to the help centre.
