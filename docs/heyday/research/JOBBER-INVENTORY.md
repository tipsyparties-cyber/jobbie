Where none of those says, the plan is left UNCONFIRMED.

- Extra users: "Add users for $29/mo each."
- Free trial: 14 days on the Grow plan, no card.
- Franchise / multi-location: custom pricing via the Partnerships team.

**Add-ons (pricing page add-on cards):** Marketing Suite **$99/mo**; Jobber AI Receptionist **$29/mo**; Pipeline **$49/mo**. All three are "Add-on" on Core, Connect and Grow, and included in Plus.
**Price conflict on Jobber's own site:** the /features/ index "Add-ons & Premium Services" block shows Marketing Suite **$79/month** and AI Receptionist **$99/month**. The pricing page is the more authoritative source.
**Reviews, Campaigns, Referrals:** each "is available as an add-on to most Jobber plans, or as part of Marketing Suite". No standalone price is published (UNCONFIRMED).

**Payment rates (same on all plans):** card processing 2.9% + 30¢; Tap to Pay 2.7% + 30¢; bank payment (ACH, US only) 1%; Instant Payouts an additional 1% fee.
